## Profile

I'd rather own whether a platform works than just have opinions on how it should. In 25 years in IT, eight of them at BEC Financial Technologies in Danish banking, I have owned roadmaps and priorities for cloud, integration and operations platforms, and worked with engineers, architects, security and consuming teams until the first systems ran in production. Today I'm Senior Cloud Architect at Lundbeck. On the side I own the infrastructure behind We Are Falcon's live-TV software, where AI agents write and test the infrastructure changes and I own the guardrails and the production accountability. I use AI thoughtfully, and human judgment stays accountable. I'm calm when things are on fire, and direct, usually with a smile.

**What I'm looking for:** real mandate over a technical platform's direction, roadmap and architecture decisions, as Platform PM/TPM, Platform Lead, Cloud/Platform/Infrastructure Architect, Cloud Platform Owner or CCoE Lead. Ideally about 60–70% direction, roadmap and stakeholders, and 30–40% hands-on close to production. People leadership is welcome but not the core.
